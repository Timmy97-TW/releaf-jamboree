New homework

Choose three to five talks, and say why.

  1. Read the whole schedule. Filter by topic, or search the words you care about.
  2. Choose three to five you are actually going to. For ReLEAF, or purely for yourself.
  3. For each one, write a sentence or two: why this one, and what you want to bring back.
  4. Press “Make my card”, copy the image, and paste it into Discord.

Due before we fly, Monday 10 November. Working it out in Paris is too late; the good rooms fill up.
