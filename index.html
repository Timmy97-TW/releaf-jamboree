02The shape of an answer · a round a day

A good answer has a shape. Learn it, then drill it.

Two students can know the same thing and score differently, because one answered the question and the other just talked. Sixty to ninety seconds, in this order:

  1. Thanks, briefly, and not every time
  2. The answer, first sentence, with a number or a name
  3. The evidence we have, named down to the figure
  4. The limit, in your own words, one clause
  5. Then the next step, and only if the wiki says it

Then drill it out loud, on a clock, until you do not need the script. The three training sites ask different questions, so do not stay on just one.

      Getting around

      Metro, tram, Navigo

      The nearest stop is Porte de Versailles – Parc des Expositions on tram T3a.

      1. From CDG: RER B towards Saint-Rémy-lès-Chevreuse to Cité Universitaire, then T3a towards Pont du Garigliano.
      2. From Orly: metro line 14 to Maison Blanche, walk 6 minutes to Porte d'Italie, then T3a.

      A Navigo Easy card costs €2 at any airport or station; load single tickets (€2.55) or a pack of ten (€25.50). The weekly Navigo Semaine (€32.40) runs Monday to Sunday.

      Good to know

      Life in Paris

      Time: Paris is 7 hours behind Taipei in November. Message home before 16:00 Paris time if you want them awake.

      Weather: November runs about 5–12 °C, often grey and drizzly, with sunset around 17:10. Bring a warm jacket and an umbrella.

      Plugs: type C/E at 230 V. Money: euro, cards widely taken. Emergency: 112.

      App: the official My iGEM App holds your ticket and the live schedule. Download it now.

        If you get separated

        Stop, call, go to the meeting point.

        1. Stop. Stay where you are, somewhere public and well lit. Don't wander off looking for the group.
        2. Call an instructor, then post in the team chat where you are.
        3. No answer in 15 minutes? Go to the meeting point: our booth during the Jamboree, the hotel lobby any other time.
        4. Phone dead or lost? Ask a shop, a hotel desk or a police officer for help, and show them the hotel address.
        5. Real danger: call 112 first, then an instructor.
