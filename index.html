        Venue

        Paris Convention Centre

        Pavilion 7, 3rd floor
        1 Place de la Porte de Versailles, 75015 Paris

        Walk in at the main street entrance on the corner of boulevard Victor and boulevard Lefebvre. Pavilion 7 is about 450 m further on, roughly 8 minutes, slightly uphill, with moving walkways.

        Google Maps Apple Maps
        Getting around

        Metro, tram, Navigo

        The nearest stop is Porte de Versailles – Parc des Expositions on tram T3a.

        1. From CDG: RER B towards Saint-Rémy-lès-Chevreuse to Cité Universitaire, then T3a towards Pont du Garigliano.
        2. From Orly: metro line 14 to Maison Blanche, walk 6 minutes to Porte d'Italie, then T3a.

        A Navigo Easy card costs €2 at any airport or station; load single tickets (€2.55) or a pack of ten (€25.50). The weekly Navigo Semaine (€32.40) runs Monday to Sunday.

        Good to know

        Life in Paris

        Time: Paris is 7 hours behind Taipei in November. Message home before 16:00 Paris time if you want them awake.

        Weather: November runs about 5–12 °C, often grey and drizzly, with sunset around 17:10. Bring a warm jacket and an umbrella.

        Plugs: type C/E at 230 V. Money: euro, cards widely taken. Emergency: 112.

        App: the official My iGEM App holds your ticket and the live schedule. It goes live shortly before the event.
